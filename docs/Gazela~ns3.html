  Gazela Primeiro
Click here to add your text.
The barketine Gazela Primeiro was built in Portugal in
1883, rebuilt in 1901 and had a long career (until 1969) as a fishing vessel sailing out of Lisbon to fish the Grand Banks area off Nova Scotia. She is 178 ft. overall, has a 17' draft, and weighs more than 600 tons!

Since the 1970s she has been the city of Philadelphia's good will "ambassador" and a living museum of ship technology.

Gazela is now at the Boothbay Harbor Shipyard, having sailed here from Philadelphia with a volunteer crew, and is undergoing much-needed repairs and maintenance.
