                       1870 US Census

Catharine Latt Philadelphia Ward 19 Dist 57 (2nd Enum), Philadelphia, PA, born abt 1846 Female  She was born Catharine Elwanger, in Wurtemburg, Germany.
 

Christian Latt Philadelphia Ward 19 Dist 57 (2nd Enum), Philadelphia, PA, born abt 1842 Male   He was born in Prussia, Germany. He was listed as a "boilermaker" in the 1900 US census.

Elizabeth Latt Philadelphia Ward 19 Dist 57 (2nd Enum), Philadelphia, PA ,born abt 1867 Female (She died at age 4 yrs. 6 mos. Her obituary is pasted into the family bible. Her mother, Catharine must have been pregnant with Emma who was born in 1871. So strange that Emma's little girl Katherine died at the same age.)


Elizabeth Latt Philadelphia Ward 19 Dist 57 (2nd Enum), Philadelphia, PA abt 1808 Female  (This must have been Christian's mother.)


Louisa Latt Philadelphia Ward 19 Dist 57 (2nd Enum), Philadelphia, PA abt 1848 Female  (Probably she was Christian's sister.)
