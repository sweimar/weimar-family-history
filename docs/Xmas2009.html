Dear Friends and Family,

We wish you every good and joyful thing this coming year!
   Here in Maine, Bob has been active at the lab, enjoying a lively new crop of researchers and post-docs with an international flavor. Bigelow is making a push to begin building on their future site in East Boothbay.  At the ol' homestead on the River Road, Bob hired a guy with a wood-splitter to help with that chore, and worked so well along with him that he offered Bob a job at $20 an hour!
   Ruth is continuing to be involved in teaching the Transcendental Meditation technique to teachers and kids at a tribal (Passamaquoddy) school in downeast Maine.  They find TM fitting in naturally with their own traditions, and are seeing positive changes in their school.  This year saw the publication of a superb Passamaquoddy/Maliseet dictionary after more than 20 years of painstaking work by tribal elder David Francis and Prof. Robert Leavitt.
   This summer, we were saddened by the passing of Bob's cousin, Sister Carol Morrison. She was a radiant, caring person and will continue to be an inspiration.
