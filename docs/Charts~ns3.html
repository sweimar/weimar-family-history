Ancestor Portraits
         These charts were collected in Germany by Ethel Weimar Southgate, daughter of  William Weimar, b.1870 in America.

Just to get you oriented,  Jakob Andreas Weimar (Chart #1) was Andrew Weimar's father. The word "backer" next to his name (with an umlaut over the a) means "baker".  No wonder some of us like pies and cakes!
Andrew's mother was Christina Flohr (Chart #2) who was Jakob's second wife..

Charts 3 and 4 are earlier ancestors of Weimars and Latts respectively.
