David Stimson, General Manager

Restoring Alera, a Herreshoff New York 30.
Alera, built in 1906, was hull #1 of 18...a lucky find by David for the shipyard! The launching was on June 25th.
David's son Abraham, a college student during the winter, is in the orange hat. His brother Nathaniel also works at the yard part-time.


In the lofting bay: David and an assistant have taken the lines off a hand-carved model of a schooner of Dave's design and transferred them to a full-sized pattern on the floor.
Recent jobs at the yard include: sandblasting and repainting an army T-boat (left, in background) now renamed the Pathfinder, to be refurbished by the new owner for tourist excursions, and repairing the rudder of the Pelagic Australis (above), an aluminum-hulled ship used for projects such as surveying the high latitudes as far as the Antarctic.
David with Billy Whiskers, an ocean-going dory he designed and built for Pine Island Camp.
The dock and harbor on a misty day; the yard has 40 moorings for its own boats and for guest boats.  It is a full-service marina with a marine railway for hauling out and launching.
